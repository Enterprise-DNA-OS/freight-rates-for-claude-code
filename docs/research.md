# Target selection and sources

Checked 15 November 2026. Quotiss belongs to the new freight-rate-management family: tariff renewal, local charges, quote margin and commercial follow-up. Existing freight forwarding builds cover shipment execution; this desk is the rate-to-quote commercial workflow. Learning operations was already built tonight and was excluded.

Selection annual bill: US$23,760 for the Plus minimum at monthly list pricing (20 accounts multiplied by US$99 per month multiplied by twelve). The vendor also lists a ten percent discount for prepaid annual subscriptions. This is a list-price scenario, not a verified NZ/AU customer invoice. No current Quotiss user was found in available customer brains or CRM snapshots. Live CRM was not accessed. Buyer fit stays low rather than inventing customer evidence.

Scores, one to five: annual bill 3; data-shaped 5; our buyers pay 1; search demand 2; one-session buildable 4; import path 4. Total 19/30. Directory pages rank for alternative searches, but no measured search volume was available. Public reviews are mostly positive; no dissatisfaction claim is made.

Toolio merchandise planning scored 1/4/1/2/4/3 = 15 without a verified public five-figure price. Freightify scored 1/4/1/2/3/3 = 14 with private pricing and live-rate dependencies. TraceGains was rejected because supplier assurance and specification families already exist and its shared network is material. No other candidate was registered.

- [Quotiss pricing](https://quotiss.com/pricing/): Starter, Plus and Pro seat plans, minimums, subscription modules, separately priced services and annual prepayment discount. Pricing was available in indexed vendor content; a direct request was rate-limited.
- [Quotiss FCL contract export](https://help.quotiss.com/help/new-how-to-download-the-rates-in-excel): downloaded this run with curl, including its public matrix example. Supports the direct FCL import layout and warns that freight surcharges and internal comments are included.
- [Quotiss rate upload](https://help.quotiss.com/help/how-to-upload-rates-automatically): different templates for FCL, LCL, air and road. Their calculations are not interchangeable.
- [Quotiss local charges](https://help.quotiss.com/help/how-to-update-port-local-charges): location, currency, container/shipment bases and other mode-specific rules.
- [Quotiss dashboard export](https://help.quotiss.com/help/how-to-export-data-from-the-dashboard): administrator export of filtered statistics to Excel. This is not a full quotation-history import.
- [Quotiss reviews and alternatives](https://www.capterra.com/p/172664/Quotiss/): a research lead for search competition, not source pricing or proof of search volume.
- [Toolio merchandise planning](https://www.toolio.com/merchandise-planning), [Freightify pricing](https://freightify.com/pricing) and [TraceGains capabilities](https://tracegains.com/capabilities/): shortlist sources.

All demo business names, carriers, people, routes, costs and quotations are fictional examples. Page command examples are captured from the executable demo. The ten questions describe this base's cross-record analysis, not unsupported limitations of the incumbent's reporting.
